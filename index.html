Trade discipline gate

Adapted conceptually from TraderMonty's trading-skill workflow: assess market regime, position sizing, pre-trade discipline, journal, and postmortem. No external repository code is executed.

Capital milestones

Paper portfolio → $40 review50%

Fixed paper-trading rules (capital growth never increases the 1% risk cap):

  1. Maximum one open position at a time.
  2. Risk no more than 1% of paper account equity per trade, including estimated costs.
  3. Predetermine a stop-loss before every entry.
  4. Never increase risk after a loss.
  5. Never chase a rapidly pumping token.
  6. Record losing trades without hiding them.
  7. Require a written reason for every trade.
  8. If agents disagree, skip the trade.

Example: $1,000 equity allows at most $10 planned loss if the stop is hit; position notional may exceed $10. Gaps and slippage can exceed planned risk. Milestones trigger review only, never automatic scaling.
